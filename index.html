How this works

Two minutes, once.
  1. Open Mission Control every day. The plan card below tells you which week you're in and what that week is about.
  2. Work the blocks top to bottom. Each Start runs a timer and opens the right tool. If you only have 45 minutes, keep the 45 min plan.
  3. In drills, say the answer out loud first, then reveal it and grade yourself honestly: 1 = right and fast, 2 = right but slow, 3 = wrong. Wrong ones go to your mistake log and come back the next day.
  4. Stuck on what something means? Every block has a Manual link. The Field Manual tab also has the resources list: which video, podcast or book to use, and when.
  5. Your progress lives only on this device. Back it up weekly: Settings → Export → Copy, paste it into a note.

Wiretap

stub
Wiretap becomes useful in week 9. Until then, do gisting from Mission Control: the Gisting block gives you a short video and three questions to answer.

Wiretap trains gisting: you hear or read a short intercept once at natural speed and report who, what, where, when and intent before the detail fades. Each intercept climbs rungs from a clean read to a noisy, half-heard one.
